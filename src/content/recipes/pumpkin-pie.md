steps:
  - Heat the oven to 400°F (205°C). Cut the top off each sugar pumpkin to remove the stem and get a flat surface, flip, cut in half, and scrape out the seeds (the seeds can be roasted separately). Lightly oil the halves, place them on a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet, and roast for 40 minutes, or until very soft and easily pricked with a fork.
  - Peel off the skins or scrape out the flesh, then blend in a food processor or blender until very smooth. If the puree is much more watery than canned pumpkin, drain it in a cheesecloth-lined metal sieve for a few minutes, or until it is the right thickness.
  - While the pumpkin roasts (or whenever you like), make the caramelized milk. In a pot with plenty of room, whisk together the whole milk, heavy cream, and sugar. Set over medium-high heat. As soon as it gets hot and slowly comes to a simmer, reduce the heat to medium and simmer gently, whisking now and then so it does not stick, until it reduces to 1 1/4 cups (300 ml), about 25 to 30 minutes. It is getting close when whisking makes it bubble hard. At room temperature, the consistency should match sweetened condensed milk. Refrigerate if you are not using it right away.
  - For the crust, add the flour, sugar, and salt to a food processor and pulse a few times to mix. Add the cold butter cubes and pulse until you have pea-sized bits throughout the flour. Move the mixture to a bowl if needed. With a fork, mix and slowly drizzle in ice water (water with ice cubes in it), then knead with your hands now and then, just until it forms a cohesive dough. Do not use a fixed amount of water; add it until the dough comes together.
  - Flatten the dough into a thick disc about 4 inches across, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for about 20 minutes, or overnight, so it can relax. The puree, caramelized milk, and dough can all be made ahead.
  - When the dough is firm but not rock hard (let it sit out briefly if needed), flour a work surface and the dough, and roll it into a rough circle about 1/4 inch thick, large enough for a 9-inch pie tin with overhang. Fold the dough in half, then in half again to make a triangle, place the tip in the center of the pan, and unfold. Trim the excess and gently crimp the edges. Chill the shaped crust so it firms up and shrinks less.
  - "Blind-bake the crust: line it with parchment (a little overhang) and weigh it down with rice, dried beans, or pie weights so the bottom stays flat. Bake at 400°F for about 15 to 18 minutes, or until the outer crust is dry and crisp but not brown. Carefully remove the weights, then bake 10 minutes more to finish the bottom. If it puffs, press it back down while it bakes. Cool the crust to room temperature on a wire rack. This can also be done a day ahead."
  - For the filling, whisk the roasted pumpkin puree with the caramelized milk (or the canned substitutes above). Add the whole eggs and egg yolk, mix, then add the salt, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg. Whisk until smooth.
  - Pour the custard into the prepared 9-inch crust. Tap the pie on the counter to flatten it and release air bubbles, and pop any bubbles that rise with a knife. Bake in an oven preheated to 325°F for 1 hour to 1 hour 10 minutes, or until the custard is completely set and a knife inserted comes out clean.
  - Serve with a dollop of whipped cream.

From [How To Turn A Whole Pumpkin Into The Best Pumpkin pie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jXGQphItWxQ) by Joshua Weissman.

One batch makes one pie in a 9-inch pie dish.

- Most of the work can be done ahead: the puree, the caramelized milk, the dough, and even the blind-baked crust.
- The filling uses 15 oz (425 g) of puree, not the entire yield of the two pumpkins. One 15 oz (425 g) can of pumpkin puree can replace the roasted puree.
- The milk mixture reduces to 1 1/4 cups (300 ml), which the video also calls 396 g. A 14 oz can of sweetened condensed milk can replace it.
- Ice water has no measured amount. Add it until the dough comes together.
- The video calls the nutmeg "one gram"; it gives no teaspoon amount.

Metric amounts from the video, for 1 batch: whole milk 828 ml, heavy cream 118 ml, sugar for the milk 230 g, flour 302 g, sugar for the crust 13 g, salt for the crust 5 g, butter 113 g, salt for the filling 2 g, ginger 2 g, cinnamon 3 g, cloves 1 g.
