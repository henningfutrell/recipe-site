steps:
  - In a small bowl, whisk together all sauce ingredients (soy sauce, oyster sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, cornstarch, and chicken broth). Set aside.
  - Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  - Add the chicken pieces in a single layer and cook for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  - Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the wok. Add garlic and ginger, stirring for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  - Add broccoli and carrots, stir-frying for 2-3 minutes until slightly tender but still crisp.
  - Add bell peppers and stir-fry for another 2 minutes.
  - Return the chicken to the wok. Pour the sauce over everything and toss to combine.
  - Cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens and coats everything evenly.
  - Serve immediately over steamed rice or noodles, garnished with sesame seeds and green onions.
---

This stir-fry is the ultimate weeknight dinner solution. By prepping all your ingredients before you start cooking, the actual cooking time is incredibly quick. The key to a great stir-fry is high heat and constant movement, which keeps the vegetables crisp-tender and prevents everything from steaming. Feel free to substitute your favorite vegetables or use what you have on hand!
